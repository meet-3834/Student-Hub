Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply?

Apply via the admissions portal or contact the admissions office for program-specific requirements.

What programs are offered?

Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Computer Applications, Sciences and doctoral programs.

Where is CHARUSAT located?

CHARUSAT is located in Changa, near Anand, Gujarat.

Are scholarships available?

Merit and need-based scholarships are available; contact the scholarship office for eligibility and deadlines.

When are admissions deadlines?

Deadlines vary by program and intake — check the admissions portal or contact admissions@charusat.edu.in.
