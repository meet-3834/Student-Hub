About CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is a multidisciplinary university located in Changa, Gujarat founded in 2009. It fosters excellence in teaching, research and innovation across Engineering, Pharmacy, Management, Computer Applications and Basic Sciences.

Schools & Programs

  • School of Engineering & Technology — B.Tech / M.Tech
  • School of Pharmacy — B.Pharm / M.Pharm
  • School of Management — BBA / MBA
  • School of Computer Applications — BCA / MCA
  • School of Sciences — MSc and Research programs

Vision & Mission

Vision: To be a centre of excellence producing skilled professionals and researchers who contribute to society and industry.

Mission: Provide quality education, strengthen research, and build partnerships for innovation and employment.

Recognition

Accredited by national bodies, active MoUs with industry partners and regular placement drives supported by the Training & Placement office.
