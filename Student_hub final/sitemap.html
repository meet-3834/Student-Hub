Portal Site Map

Complete hierarchical overview of all pages across the student portal.

🏛️ Main Information Pages

  • Home — University introduction, highlights, and dynamic events feed.
  • About — Institutional details, leadership, and academic programs.
  • Contact — Campus locations, admissions contact info, and inquiry form.
  • FAQ (Practical 6) — University FAQs dynamically rendered from faqs.json with search and accordion filter.

🎓 Dynamic Practical 6 Data Modules

  • Events Directory — Dynamic event cards rendered from events.json with live search, category filter, sorting, and pagination.
  • Student Profiles Directory — Dynamic student cards from students.json featuring Intermediate Dependent Location Dropdowns (Country → State → City) and CGPA sorting.
  • Dashboard — Aggregated real-time metrics and dynamic preview cards fetched via Fetch API.

👤 Student & Account Services

  • Profile — Individual student profile information.
  • Feedback — Student suggestions and grievance form.
  • Login — Student and faculty authentication portal.
  • Register (Practical 5) — Account registration form with real-time field validation and password strength indicator.
